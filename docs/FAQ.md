# EasyTrack – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. What is EasyTrack?
EasyTrack is a simple personal expense tracker that helps users record, organize, and monitor their daily expenses.

## 2. How can I add a new expense?
Navigate to the expense form, enter the required information, such as the expense name, amount, category, and date, then submit the form.

## 3. Can I edit an existing expense?
Yes. Find the expense you want to update, select the edit option, modify the necessary information, and save your changes.

## 4. Can I delete an expense?
Yes. Users can select the delete option to remove an expense record.

## 5. How can I monitor my spending?
You can use the summary section to view your total expenses and transaction information.

## 6. Can I organize expenses by category?
Yes. EasyTrack allows users to organize and view expenses according to their categories.

## 7. How can I monitor my budget?
Navigate to the budget section to set a budget and monitor your remaining balance.

## 8. Can I search for a specific transaction?
Yes. Use the available search and filtering features to find specific expense records.

## 9. Who can use EasyTrack?
EasyTrack is designed for individuals who want a simple way to manage and monitor their personal expenses.
